# Borderless MVP Definition

## Phase I: Prove the Network

Based on the Strategy Paper, Product Requirements Document, and Operating & Business Blueprint, August 2026.

## Product Definition

Borderless is an integrated cross-border trade platform. Its long-term role is to connect the fragmented layers around a trade transaction:

```text
Discover -> Verify -> Match & Negotiate -> Transact -> Finance -> Prepare -> Move -> Settle -> Learn
```

Phase I is a **managed marketplace focused on Market Access + Trust**. It is not a passive catalogue and it is not yet a complete transaction, logistics, finance, or intelligence platform. Human intervention is deliberately part of the MVP so Borderless can learn the workflow before automating it.

## MVP Objective

Prove that Borderless can build a trusted network of businesses and consistently create qualified trade opportunities between buyers and suppliers.

The first success condition is that businesses join because they can find trusted trading partners.

## Initial Market and Users

- Agriculture and agricultural trade participants.
- Narrow product categories and trade corridors.
- Buyers/importers, suppliers/exporters, manufacturers, processors, commodity traders, sourcing staff, verification staff, and administrators.

## Phase I Capabilities

### 1. Verified Business Profiles

- Register a business, select a role, and add markets, categories, and company information.
- Submit verification information and display verification status.
- Support manual review, approval, rejection, and request-for-information states.

### 2. Discovery and Product Listings

- Search products, companies, and RFQs.
- Filter by country, category, role, and verification status.
- Publish structured agricultural listings with origin, specifications, availability, quantity, images, and supporting information.
- Edit, pause, archive, and moderate listings.

### 3. RFQs and Responses

- Create RFQs with product, quantity, origin/destination, timing, specifications, and commercial requirements.
- Target selected or relevant suppliers.
- Submit, review, and compare supplier responses and terms.
- Track RFQ status and keep communication connected to the request.

### 4. Managed Sourcing

Receive a requirement, search the network, identify and qualify counterparties, verify businesses, present options, introduce parties, support RFQs/quotations, and record the opportunity outcome.

### 5. Opportunities and Messaging

The opportunity record connects buyer, supplier, product, RFQ, messages, Borderless interventions, current status, next action, and outcome.

```text
New -> Sourcing -> Matched -> Verified -> Introduced -> RFQ -> Negotiation -> Qualified
```

Phase I supports buyer-supplier conversations, operational notes, unread notifications, and secure attachments when storage is available.

### 6. Admin and Analytics

- Manage users and profiles.
- Review verification submissions.
- Moderate products and RFQs.
- Assign sourcing tasks and manage opportunities.
- Review suspicious activity.
- Measure registration, verification, listing, search, RFQ, response, introduction, negotiation, qualification, and repeat activity events.

## Buyer Experience

```text
Register -> Profile -> Verify -> Discover suppliers/products -> Review trust
-> Create RFQ -> Receive responses -> Message -> Compare -> Qualify opportunity
```

## Exporter Experience

```text
Register -> Profile -> Verify -> Publish products -> Discover buyer demand
-> Respond to RFQ -> Message -> Qualify opportunity
```

The journeys converge around a shared opportunity record. Phase I ends at trusted discovery, matching, early negotiation, and qualification.

## Explicitly Out of Phase I

- Full transaction execution, purchase orders, and contracts.
- Escrow or protected payment.
- Integrated freight, customs, warehousing, insurance, and shipment tracking.
- Trade finance, FX, and insurance products.
- Advanced intelligence, forecasting, risk scoring, benchmarking, and AI sourcing.
- Multi-sector expansion before agricultural liquidity is proven.

## Success Metrics

- Verified businesses, active buyers, and active suppliers.
- Qualified opportunities, RFQs, and RFQ response rate.
- Accepted introductions, negotiations started, repeat users, and time to qualified match.

## Roadmap

- **Phase 0:** Manually facilitate real transactions and learn the workflow.
- **Phase I:** Market Access + Trust.
- **Phase II:** Quotes, purchase orders, contracts, documents, milestones, and payment coordination.
- **Phase III:** Freight, customs, transport, warehousing, insurance, and tracking.
- **Phase IV:** Purchase-order finance, invoice finance, working capital, FX, and insurance partners.
- **Phase V:** Pricing, market intelligence, risk, AI sourcing, forecasting, and analytics.

## Operating Principles

- Create liquidity before creating complexity.
- Trust before scale.
- Start with the transaction.
- Own the customer relationship and workflow; integrate ecosystem infrastructure.
- Build with the ecosystem, not against it.
- Revenue should follow valuable or completed trade activity.
